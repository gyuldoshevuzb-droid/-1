# Anima Survivors: Reborn v0.3 — Premium Combat Pass

## Rendering
- Desktop renderer promoted to Forward+; Android uses Godot Mobile renderer.
- Filmic tonemapping, glow, SSAO, fog, emissive weapon materials and dynamic lights.
- Ambient particle field and distant procedural mountain silhouettes make arenas read as larger biomes.

## Character feel
- Native CharacterBody3D acceleration/deceleration retained and enhanced with speed-driven body stretch, directional lean and movement bob.
- Spring-damped combat camera with movement lead and impact/dash shake.
- Dash now leaves emissive afterimages and burst particles while preserving invulnerability frames.
- Footstep dust gives movement contact with the ground.

## Combat VFX
- GPU spark bursts on hits, crits, dashes and deaths.
- Stronger slash flashes, lightning impact bursts, shock rings and critical damage feedback.
- Enemy hit squash/recovery and larger boss-death effects.

## Release targets
- Android APK export preset upgraded to version 0.3.0.
- Windows preset retained.
- HTML v9.1 remains the legacy fallback build; Godot Reborn is the premium branch.
