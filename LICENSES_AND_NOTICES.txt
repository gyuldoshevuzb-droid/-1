POLICY FOR GOOGLE PLAY RELEASES
- Accept by default: project-original, CC0, MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause.
- Attribution-required assets must have their notice included before release.
- Reject: unclear license, "free download" without license, personal-use-only, editorial-only, ripped/extracted assets, trademarks used as game content without permission, and assets copied from other games.
- A file is not release-approved merely because it is free of charge.

This notice is included in the in-game Licenses screen.
