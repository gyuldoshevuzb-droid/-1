# Anima Survivors: Reborn v0.5 — SPECTACLE

## Animation overhaul
- Reworked procedural character locomotion into layered pose animation.
- Added breathing, torso counter-rotation, head stabilization, coat/sash secondary motion and stronger dash body lean.
- Added distinct attack, cast, hit-react and dash pose layers.
- Weapon actions now drive upper-body animation instead of sharing one generic recoil.
- Enemy rigs now have animated arms, hit reactions, melee anticipation and boss/ranged casting poses.
- Melee enemies use a short readable wind-up before damage is applied.
- Enemy death now has a short physical collapse/disintegration animation instead of instant removal.

## Weapon VFX identity pass
- Blade: persistent orbital streaks and contact arc sweeps.
- Void Shard: muzzle flash, crystal projectile silhouette, hard energy trail and bespoke impact.
- Eclipse Ring: rune circles, layered rings and radial aura burst.
- Crimson Whip: curved double-layer sweep, sparks and terminal shock ring.
- Spirit Owls: wing-shaped launch VFX, spirit projectile silhouette and wispy trails.
- Thunder: ground sigils, chained strikes and more readable lightning impact language.

## New Anima Burst system
- New ultimate meter charged by kills and bosses.
- Press Q on desktop or ANIMA on touchscreen when full.
- Sakura: Blade Storm — radial execution field.
- Kaito: Void Bloom — multi-target void barrage.
- Luna: Eclipse — shield, slow and large lunar blast.
- Ren: Crimson Cyclone — high-damage close-range storm plus temporary fury.
- Added dedicated HUD meter and mobile ability button.

## Combat presentation
- Added new procedural VFX primitives: rune circle, aura burst, arc sweep, orbit trail, whip arc, void bloom, eclipse, crimson cyclone, thunder sigil and styled projectile trails.
- Boss and ranged enemy casts now have stronger telegraphs.
- Improved critical moment readability while keeping all effects self-contained and asset-free.
